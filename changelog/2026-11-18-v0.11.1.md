One change to 0.11.0: **a message from another agent in herdr-core now has a
teal bar and a teal tab**, so it stands apart from what you typed.

<!-- truncate -->

## Changed

- **A sub-agent's message in herdr-core has a teal bar.** A block with a tab
  (a message handed over as `<hc from="…">…</hc>`) now has a teal bar and a
  teal tab, so it stands apart from what you typed, whose bar stays gray. With
  `NO_COLOR` only the tab line tells them apart.
